WeBuddhist is a nonprofit with three platforms for Buddhist practice, study, and creative work, supported by WeBuddhist Tech. The three platforms and their core tools remain free forever. Each includes free onboarding and limited support. Optional paid services support long-term sustainability.

AI assistants include a free token allowance. Tokens measure the amount of content an AI model processes or generates. Additional token usage is charged according to the model and the type of assistant used.

## WeBuddhist Practice Platform

An app-first platform for daily Buddhist practice, learning, and community, with a simplified web version.

### Free platform and tools

Includes the app and simplified web version, Practice Spaces, studio tools, free onboarding, limited support, and a free AI token allowance.

### Five sections in the app

- **HOME:** Relevant daily practices and content, personal plans, community calendars and updates, reminders, upcoming or live events, and accumulation progress.
- **LEARN:** Short-form content feeds and a text reader that support practice with inspiration and information.
- **PRACTICE:** A mala, timer, and accumulation counter; chants, prayers, sadhanas, meditation and guided meditation; practice plans and live group practice with texts.
- **CONNECT:** A Practice Spaces page to find and join spaces, and a Community page to make friends and follow other people.
- **ME:** Personal practice history, streaks, notes, bookmarks, and settings.

### Practice spaces and studio

Any individual, teacher, or community can create their own public or private Practice Space for free. It is their place on WeBuddhist to bring people together, publish content, and organize practice. Each space includes:

- **Community:** A calendar, notifications, SMS and email, a store, and a posts and announcements feed.
- **Practice:** Chant collections, practice plans, live events, prayer requests, members, and personal or group accumulations. Plans can combine texts, meditation, instructions, readings, videos, daily activities, and accumulation goals.
- **Learning:** Text, quote, story, and art collections, plus a video feed.

The studio lets space owners create and edit content, manage members, invitations, roles and permissions, and configure social media links or integrations.

### How content reaches the app

Content published in a space also reaches the wider WeBuddhist community through shared channels. Public plans, chants, meditations, and live practice content appear in Practice; public texts, quotes, stories, teaching videos, and art appear in Learn, alongside content from other spaces. Events also surface in Home and Connect. Private content remains subject to the space's access controls.

### Accessible practice and source grounded AI

- **Synchronized recitation:** Live text follows the chant master at in-person, online, or hybrid events, including text overlays on livestreams. Original, translated, bilingual, and transliterated text supports participation in the user's script, with optional prerecorded spoken translation through one earphone.
- **Text reader and practice assistant:** Passages link to translations, commentaries, dictionaries, and teachings. Communities select sources and multilingual displays; practice Q&A is grounded in those sources and their lineage or tradition.

### Optional paid services

- **Additional AI usage:** Token usage beyond the free allowance is charged according to the model and assistant type.
- **Practice content preparation:** OCR, proofreading, translation, transliteration, audio, reference linking, and complete practice or event packages.
- **Community commerce:** Stores can sell products, books, consultations, temple services, and offerings, with transaction fees. Donations use communities' external payment channels with no WeBuddhist commission.

## WeBuddhist Academy

A free platform built on Open edX and customized for Buddhist communities. Any community can create a campus, build courses, manage students, and issue certificates.

### Free platform and tools

Includes the campus, core learning and course studio tools, free onboarding, limited support, and a free AI token allowance.

### Buddhist learning features

- **Language and texts:** Full Tibetan localization and support for other Buddhist-community languages. A reader combines preloaded canonical texts, translations, bilingual or transliterated display, and teacher-curated commentaries, dictionaries, references, and related teachings.
- **AI support:** A Buddhist course creation assistant, a student chatbot, and text-specific agents for providers and students. Tutors use course texts, lectures, and selected references for Q&A, clarification, comprehension checks, revision, and assessment preparation.
- **Live teaching:** Live course streams and synchronized classroom reading: students' screens follow the teacher's selected passage online or offline, with access to translations and references.
- **Core learning tools:** Structured lessons, video, audio, readings, exercises, quizzes, assessments, discussions, progress tracking, and student management. Campuses manage their identity, courses, teachers, students, sources, and pricing.

### Course studio

- **Studio:** Buddhist tools for easy access to texts, translations, and references, with AI assistants to create courses, grade exercises, and prepare lesson or lecture plans based on student performance and questions.
- **Lecture conversion:** Turn recorded teachings into structured courses through transcription, topic identification, short learning segments, summaries, references, comprehension questions, and assessments. Course design tools also support objectives, activities, translation, and adaptation.

### Campus sales and payments

- **Course income:** Campuses choose free or paid courses, set their own prices, charge students, and receive payments for full courses, selected portions, or certificates. The Academy platform and core tools remain free.

### Optional paid services

- **Additional AI usage:** Token usage beyond the free allowance is charged according to the model and assistant type.
- **Course creation assistance and training:** When needed, campuses can pay WeBuddhist for curriculum design, media processing, exercises, translation, formatting, course setup, and training in course creation or platform use. These services are optional; onboarding and limited support remain free.
- **Specialized data and AI services:** Optional paid text or course packages combine editions, translations, commentaries, teachings, terminology, and research with a reusable AI assistant.
- **Custom learning systems:** Premium white-label academies with their own domain, branding, catalog, languages, and custom features, including Buddhist data and AI, hosting, maintenance, updates, and support.

## Buddhist Creators Platform

A platform for Buddhist creators featuring competitions, event and community announcements, a portfolio directory, and a Creator Studio with three assistants.

### Free platform and tools

Includes the creator network, portfolio directory, announcements, core Creator Studio tools, free onboarding, limited support, and a free AI token allowance.

- **Competitions:** Picture, video, painting, and other creative competitions.
- **Events:** Buddhist creators conventions, Buddhist influencer awards, local creators clubs, and workshops.
- **Announcements:** Event announcements, Buddhist content buyer announcements, and creator announcements.
- **Marketplace and matchmaking:** Creators showcase and sell art, products, and services, receive commissions, and connect with content buyers. Communities find creators to promote courses, events, and practice spaces or commission content.
- **Creator Clubs:** Structured training in storytelling, scripts, photography, shooting, editing, short-form video, social media, audience development, and reliable Buddhist sourcing. Conventions provide training, networking, collaboration, and a marketplace.

### Creator Studio and its three assistants

- **Buddhist content editor assistant:** Transcribes, summarizes, splits, extracts clips from, and reorganizes teaching videos and audio. Translates and adapts long teachings into social content for different audiences and formats, including batch processing.
- **Buddhist creator assistant:** Finds reliable scriptures, commentaries, teachings, and authentic citations; supports research and source verification; develops ideas, scripts, voiceovers, and clips; translates or adapts content; and suggests collaborations.
- **Buddhist teacher assistant:** Researches topics, canonical passages, commentaries, stories, examples, and quotes; searches previous teachings; structures talks and outlines; and creates slides and visuals or adapts existing teaching materials.

### Optional paid services

- **Additional AI usage:** Token usage beyond the free allowance is charged according to the model and assistant type. Subscriptions or credits support higher usage.
- **Advanced processing:** Paid options support larger files, batch processing, and advanced transcription, translation, and adaptation.
- **Marketplace and event commerce:** Commercial sales through the platform, marketplaces, and events carry transaction or service fees.

## WeBuddhist Tech

The data, AI, API, and software foundation supporting all three platforms and their studios. It also serves communities, developers, universities, libraries, archives, and researchers through data, AI, and software services.

### Free use within the platforms

WeBuddhist Tech powers the free core tools across Practice, Academy, and Creators. AI assistants include a free token allowance; each platform includes free onboarding and limited support. Custom data work, external API services, and software delivery are described separately below.

### Two WeBuddhist innovations

- **Specialist agent memory:** A curated, structured body of Buddhist knowledge prepared for a specific text, teacher, author, topic, or tradition. It brings together root texts, editions, translations, commentaries, teachings, terminology, research, and links between relevant passages. This memory gives an AI agent a defined set of sources to consult.
- **Specialist Buddhist agent:** An AI assistant built on a specialist agent memory and configured for its subject and selected sources. It uses that memory to answer questions, explain passages, compare commentaries, and assist with practice, teaching, study, or content creation, with source references and alignment to the selected tradition or teacher.

For example, a memory for The Way of the Bodhisattva can bring together the root text, translations, commentaries, and teachings. The specialist Buddhist agent built on it can support a practice question, an Academy course, a creator's script, or an external application. The same prepared knowledge is reusable across products.

### Shared capabilities and human expertise

Text and manuscript recognition, speech transcription, translation, text alignment, and search turn Buddhist resources into usable data. Knowledge graphs record relationships between resources, while retrieval tools find relevant passages. Buddhist data specialists, scholars, monastics, and translators curate, proofread, annotate, and evaluate the material.

### Buddhist data services

- **Training datasets:** Preparation of foundational training datasets and fine-tuning datasets.
- **Specialist agent memory preparation:** Custom preparation of texts, editions, translations, commentaries, teachings, dictionaries, research, and links. Services include digitization, proofreading, translation alignment, metadata, passage-level commentary linking, and knowledge graphs.
- **API access:** Access to Buddhist texts, translations, references, and other data resources through software interfaces.

### Buddhist AI as a service

- **Specialist Buddhist agents:** Custom agents built on specialist agent memories for a text, author, teacher, topic, or tradition, with source-grounded answers and references.
- **Additional AI usage:** Token usage beyond the free allowance is charged according to the model and the type of assistant involved.
- **Custom AI APIs:** Access to AI fine-tuned or aligned in knowledge and values with a specific master, collection, or text.
- **WeBuddhist API services:** Integration of text retrieval, semantic search, translation, OCR, speech-to-text, terminology, specialist Q&A, and tradition-specific sources into other applications. An API layer can add Buddhist grounding, values alignment, and filtering to commercial or open-source models.
- **Custom AI delivery:** Paid setup, fine-tuning, dedicated or private deployments, and subscriptions or usage-based API access.

### Buddhist software as a service

- **Community software:** Custom calendars, temple apps, digital libraries, scripture readers, practice accumulation websites, community platforms, and AI assistants.
- **Organizational tools:** Custom tooling for organizations working on Buddhist data, such as manuscript transcription and annotation tools.
- **Software delivery and support:** Design, development, data and AI integration, hosting, maintenance, and support for custom software.

## Free Access and Long-Term Sustainability

WeBuddhist sustains its free platforms through optional professional services, additional AI usage, custom data and software work, and commercial transaction fees. Campuses receive payments for their courses. Donations remain external, with no WeBuddhist commission.
